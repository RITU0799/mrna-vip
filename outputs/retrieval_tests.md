## 2. Retrieval Evaluation

### 2.1 Refrigerated Storage

**Question:** What refrigerated storage temperature is required?

- **Top result:** Section 1 — Storage and Handling Requirements.
- **Cosine similarity:** 0.5558.
- **Ranking score:** 0.6544.
- **Evaluation:** The top-ranked passage contains the storage-temperature information, including refrigerated storage between 2°C and 8°C.

**Outcome:** The expected source section ranked first.

### 2.2 High-Dose Secondary Review

**Question:** When does a high dose require secondary review?

- **Top result:** Section 2 — Dosing Guidance by Age Band.
- **Cosine similarity:** 0.5063.
- **Ranking score:** 0.6159.
- **Evaluation:** Section 2 ranked first. Section 3 ranked second, with a close ranking score of 0.6137.

**Outcome:** The expected source section ranked first, but the close scores demonstrate that ranking scores alone do not establish factual correctness.

### 2.3 Monitoring Period

**Question:** Which patients require a longer monitoring period?

- **Top result:** Section 3 — Contraindications and Warnings.
- **Intent bonus:** 0.0800.
- **Ranking score:** 0.4461.
- **Source verification:** The retrieved passage specifies at least 15 minutes of monitoring after administration, or 30 minutes for patients with a documented history of any allergic reaction.

**Outcome:** Inspection of the retrieved passage confirmed that both monitoring durations were present.

### 2.4 Adverse-Event Documentation

**Question:** What adverse events must be documented within 24 hours?

- **Top result:** Section 4 — Adverse Event Documentation.
- **Cosine similarity:** 0.7609.
- **Ranking score:** 0.7842.
- **Source verification:** The top-ranked passage states that moderate or severe adverse events must be documented within 24 hours, including onset time, duration, severity, and treatment administered.

**Outcome:** The expected source section ranked first and contained the relevant documentation requirement.

### 2.5 Temperature Excursion

**Question:** What happens after a temperature excursion?

- **Rank 1:** Section 1 — Storage and Handling Requirements.
- **Rank 1 intent bonus:** 0.0500.
- **Rank 1 score:** 0.5090.
- **Rank 2:** Section 1 — Storage and Handling Requirements.
- **Rank 2 intent bonus:** 0.1200.
- **Rank 2 score:** 0.2652.

The two retrieved passages contain these source instructions:

1. Excursions above -15°C for formulations requiring ultra-low storage must be documented and reviewed before the affected batch is approved for patient use.
2. Deviations lasting longer than one hour require the batch to be quarantined pending quality review.

**Outcome:** Both required actions were confirmed in the top two retrieved passages. The dedicated temperature-excursion regression test passed.

## 4. Interpretation and Limitations

- Cosine similarity measures semantic closeness, not factual correctness.
- The ranking score combines semantic similarity, lexical relevance, and explicitly defined intent bonuses.
- Intent bonuses are heuristic rules and may not generalize to unseen queries.
- A high ranking score does not guarantee that a passage fully answers a question.
- The temperature-excursion regression test verifies that both required actions appear among the top two retrieved passages.
- The monitoring-period passage was inspected to confirm the presence of both monitoring durations.
- The evaluation covers a small set of predefined questions. Additional queries and edge cases should be tested before drawing broader conclusions.
- Recorded latency values describe a single run and may vary with hardware, caching, model loading, and system load.
- Retrieval tests do not independently verify the scientific, clinical, or regulatory accuracy of the source material.
